Lokalola is an integrated, community-based organic waste processing system that converts local organic waste into renewable energy and useful products, helping communities process their waste independently.

Waste protocol

Three lanes, one simple rule: sort at home.

  • Wet Food waste 35 kg/day, blue bin Biodigester Fermented without oxygen into biogas and digestate.
  • Dry Leaves, twigs etc. 15 kg/day, brown bin Pyrolysis Heated without oxygen into biochar.
  • Not organic Plastics & others Not processed by the unit Rejected Kept out so it never disturbs the process.
What comes out

Waste in. Heat, fertiliser and measured data out.

  • Biogas as a heat source

    3.5–4.7 m³/day

    Replaces LPG: about 200 cylinders of 3 kg a year.

  • Biochar & fertiliser

    17 kg solid + 60 L liquid per day

    A clear product to sell to nearby farmers.

  • Measured data

    IoT operating log

    Temperature, pH and gas flow are logged automatically, so the energy benefit can be proven.

Locally managed

Locally manage for greater energy.

Processing waste closer to where it is generated turns a growing problem into a local resource.

  • Less accumulation Organic waste is processed daily near the source instead of piling up and smelling.
  • Local sustainable energy Biogas from local waste becomes heat used on site.
  • Community independence Residents and waste managers stay in charge of their own facility.
