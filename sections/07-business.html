Business model

We sell the unit, then maintain and upgrade it.

Two revenue streams from Year 1: a unit sale, and a service subscription that keeps every facility running and improving.

Stream 1 · from Year 1

Unit sales

IDR 85 M per unit

  • Sold to district governments, processors and communities
  • One unit serves about 150 households on roughly 8 × 5 m of land
  • Hands-on support after the sale
Stream 2 · from Year 1

Service subscription

IDR 0.5–1 M Service Basic

  • Remote IoT monitoring
  • A visit every 3 months
  • Spare parts & repairs at 20% for Basic subscribers
Strategy insight

We sell the certain thing: the facility.

Reselling biochar and biogas is uncertain, so we don't bet on it. The buyer gets a real facility, and afterwards only spare parts and repairs.

  • A clear output. The unit makes fertiliser automatically, so owners get a ready-to-sell product instead of abstract raw biochar that could invite competition.

  • Smart inside, simple outside. IoT handles temperature, timer and safety. The operator just presses one START button.
