Why it's different

Not another waste facility.

A typical facility makes waste disappear somewhere else. Lokalola makes it useful, right where it is.

Aspect Typical facility Lokalola
Where it sits Far from home; waste is trucked out of town. In the district or village where the waste is born, on ~8 × 5 m.
What goes in Mixed waste, and organics are often turned away. Sorted at home: food waste to the biodigester, leaves and twigs to pyrolysis.
What comes out Compost at best, often nothing at all. Heat, fertiliser and biochar, every day.
Energy Released into the air as methane or smoke. Captured as biogas, stored and used on site: ≈ 200 LPG cylinders a year.
Operation Manual work that needs skilled staff. One START button. Temperature, timer and safety are automatic.
Proof Nothing is measured. Every number logged by IoT: temperature, pH, gas flow.
For the owner A running cost, starting with hauling. ≈ +IDR 2.0–2.65 M net per month (design estimate).
After the sale You're on your own. Monitoring, a visit every 3 months, spare parts and upgrades.

“Typical facility” describes common conventional set-ups; individual facilities vary. Lokalola figures are design estimates to be validated in the pilot.
