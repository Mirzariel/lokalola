The story behind Lokalola

  1. 01 Yogyakarta, every day

    282 tons

    of waste are collected every day. Roughly 40% of it is organic: food scraps, leaves, market leftovers.

  2. 02 What actually gets processed

    Only 73 tons

    The capacity exists, 148 tons a day, but supply and demand never meet.

  3. 03 The rest

    Has nowhere to go.

    Transfer stations now refuse organic waste. It piles up, it smells, it spreads disease. Then it gets burned.

  4. 04 Yet inside it

    ≈13.3 MWh

    of energy a day, locked in Yogyakarta's food waste alone. It isn't rubbish. It's energy in the wrong place.

  5. 05 So we built

    A machine that sets it free.

    Right where the waste is born: one unit per district or village, run by the community, with one START button.
