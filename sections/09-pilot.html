MVP & pilot

Minimum viable product: one Lokalola 50 kg package with a measurable energy chain.

The MVP is not an app. It is a single unit closing one target district's waste stream end-to-end: waste in, measured heat, fertiliser out.

  • Serves ≈150 households
  • Land ~8 × 5 m
  • Feedstock per day 35 kg wet + 15 kg dry
  • Fertiliser per day ~17 kg solid + ~60 L liquid

Pilot flow

  1. 1

    Waste from one target district

    Wet kitchen waste and dry waste (leaves, twigs) from about 150 households.

  2. 2

    Processing: Lokalola unit, 50 kg package

    The biodigester makes biogas, the biogas heats the pyrolysis. The heat used is logged.

  3. 3

    Fertiliser for local farmers

    About 17 kg solid and 60 L liquid fertiliser a day, sold to farmers near the site.

What we will measure

Energy Source → storage → measured use
  • m³ biogas per day
  • MJ of heat used
  • Pyrolysis temperature and holding time
Product What farmers actually buy
  • kg of fertiliser produced
  • Fertiliser quality
  • Farmer purchase price

Beyond money

Three non-financial things we need to make the pilot happen.

  • One partner district or village willing to host the pilot.
  • Access to a testing lab for fertiliser quality.
  • Energy & carbon mentors from Pertamina.

Pilot readiness path

Follows the funding lines: from bench test to pilot package.

  1. Bench test IDR 8 M

    Test the process on the bench first.

  2. Lab tests IDR 3 M

    Check fertiliser quality in a testing lab.

  3. One 50 kg pilot package IDR 65 M

    Install at the partner district, then measure biogas, heat and fertiliser.
